Mathematical Formulations & Justifications

Whitepaper Standards

1. Composite Driver Safety Score (0 - 100)

Safety Score = 100 - min(100, 2.2·HBR100 + 1.8·RAR100 + 2.0·HCR100 + 0.35·(100 - SCS) + 0.1·NightPct)
• HBR100 (Harsh Braking Rate): Decelerations with ay ≤ -3.0 m/s² normalized per 100 km. Heavy weight (2.2) because sudden stopping on two-wheelers is the leading precursor to front-wheel lockup and rear-end collisions.
• RAR100 (Rapid Acceleration Rate): Accelerations with ay ≥ +2.8 m/s² per 100 km. Reflects aggressive throttle twisting, tire slippage, and high fuel/energy wastage.
• HCR100 (Harsh Cornering Rate): Lateral acceleration |ax| ≥ 3.0 m/s² or high yaw rate |gyroz| ≥ 40°/s at speed > 20 km/h. High weight because excessive lean at speed risks low-side crashes.
• SCS (Speed Compliance Score): Penalizes continuous driving >50 km/h (0.8×) and severe overspeeding >65 km/h (2.5×) in dense delivery zones.

2. Composite Vehicle Health Index (VHI) (0 - 100)

VHI = 100 - (40·Norm(VibRMS) + 25·Norm(GyroJitter) + 20·Norm(BrakeJudder) + 10·Norm(DaysService) + 5·Norm(OdoKM))
• Chassis Vibration RMS (VibRMS): Root Mean Square of vertical acceleration deviation: √( 1/N · Σ (az,t - 9.81)² ). Directly isolates suspension damper degradation, blown fork seals, and chassis fatigue.
• Rotational Jitter (GyroJitter): Sum of σ(gyrox) + σ(gyroy) during straight cruising (v > 20 km/h, |ax| < 1.0 m/s²). Isolates steering head bearing play, rim bend, or unbalanced front tire wobble.
• Braking Judder (BrakeJudder): Vertical standard deviation during active braking (ay < -1.5 m/s²). Isolates warped brake rotors and glazed friction pads.
• Service & Mileage Aging: Normalized days since last preventative maintenance and total cumulative odometer wear.

Key Telematics Assumptions & Sensor Calibration

Physics Constraints
  • Phone Mount vs. Pocket Calibration: Mobile phone sensors mounted rigidly on handlebars capture true high-frequency chassis vibration (az). Pocket-worn phones experience soft tissue damping. The scoring pipeline detects mount type and normalizes vibration coefficients accordingly.
  • Sampling Frequency & Aggregation: Minute-level telemetry captures 1-minute time windows. Dynamic G-spikes (ax, ay, az) capture maximum sustained maneuver forces.
  • Gravity Compensation: Base gravity (9.81 m/s²) is subtracted from the orthogonal Z-axis vector to compute dynamic road shock.
  • Vehicle Independence in Driver Scoring: Driver scores are normalized per 100 km distance traveled to ensure fair scoring regardless of whether a rider covers 80 km or 250 km.
  • Non-Linearity in Wear Dynamics: Vehicle health degradation assumes mechanical wear accelerates non-linearly past threshold limits (σvib > 2.5 m/s²).
